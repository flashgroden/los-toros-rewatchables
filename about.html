What are we doing here?

Los Toros is not about deciding which movies are best. It’s about discovering which movies are worth coming back to—and why.

The Hopper

One member makes the case.

Any core member can nominate any film. Great, terrible, canonical, disreputable — doesn't matter. If a second member backs it, it qualifies for the Queue.

The Queue

Two people want it back.

The next member in the rotation chooses from qualified films. The future AI Commissioner will help rank the Queue for variety without overruling the picker.

The Watch

In person or async.

Core members commit to at least one club film per month. Async selections have a two-week window. Three of five core members make an official group result.

The Reveal

Score first. Argue second.

Craft, Emotional Impact, Rewatchability and Overall. In person, scores stay private until reveal. Async viewers don't see results until they've submitted their own.

The four numbers

Craft — how well the film executes the craft of cinema.

Emotional Impact — how it lands emotionally.

Rewatchability — not just “is it good?” but “do you want to live with it again?”

Overall — bottom line: how much do you love it?

Canon is not “best.”

A film can be brilliant and still not be something the group wants to revisit. Canon is the record of official Los Toros screenings that clear both gates: average Overall ≥ 7 and average Rewatchability ≥ 7, with at least three core-member ratings.

Separately, the Board keeps a Best Rated list so admired-but-not-rewatchable films still get their due.
